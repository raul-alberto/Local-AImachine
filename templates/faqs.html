About

A private AI assistant that runs on your machine.

Court is a local, uncensored AI assistant for creative work, coding and writing. It talks to Ollama on your own hardware, so prompts and replies never leave your computer.

Built by Nox.

What is Court?

Court is an AI companion that helps with creative ideas, coding, writing and more. It is designed to be useful and unfiltered for local, personal work.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Court runs locally through Ollama and sends nothing to external servers. Accounts, chats and indexed documents live on this machine, and you can wipe them at any time from Settings.

How do I use it?

Type your question in the composer at the bottom of the chat and press Enter. Replies stream in as they are generated, with code blocks formatted and copyable. Use Stop to cancel a reply in progress.

Can I start new conversations?

Yes. Use New chat in the sidebar. Each conversation is independent, appears under Recent chats, and can be renamed, tagged or deleted from its ⋯ menu.

How does local document search work?

Open Settings and index text, code or PDF files under Local knowledge. When retrieval is enabled, Court pulls the most relevant passages into the prompt and cites them under each answer with View sources.

What does ephemeral mode mean?

In Ephemeral privacy mode, chats stay in your session only and are cleared when you log out. Persistent mode keeps them for your account on this device.

Court is local software — responses come from the model installed on your machine and can be inaccurate.
